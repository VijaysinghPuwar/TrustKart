# Asset sources and attribution

Every product and category photo in `frontend/public/images/` comes from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) under a license that
permits reuse: public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA. Images were downloaded
once, resized and converted to WebP (800 px and 400 px), and are served from
this repository. Nothing is hotlinked.

Photos were chosen per product and checked by eye. Many products have no
freely licensed photo of the exact model, so each image records how closely
it matches:

| Match | Meaning | Shown to shoppers |
|---|---|---|
| exact | This model (or the same model in another capacity or board-partner design) | no label |
| product line | Same product line or an earlier generation | "Image shows a similar model" |
| representative | Same kind of product, may be another brand | "Representative image" |

Changes made to the originals: resizing, WebP conversion and, where noted,
cropping (for example to remove a video channel logo or background). CC BY-SA
images remain under their original license. Their authors are credited below
and on each product page.

The TrustKart logo (`frontend/public/brand/`, favicons) is project-owned.
Fonts: Inter and JetBrains Mono (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted via
Fontsource.

TrustKart is a portfolio simulation. Product names and photos are used to
describe technology products; no affiliation with or endorsement by any brand
is implied, and nothing is offered for sale.
